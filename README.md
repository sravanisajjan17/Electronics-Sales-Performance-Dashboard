# 📊 Electronics Sales Performance KPI Dashboard

A comprehensive end-to-end data analysis and visualization project analyzing electronic sales performance across multiple regions and product categories using Power BI.

---

## 📌 Project Overview

This project focuses on analyzing sales performance data in the electronics domain to uncover trends, identify top-performing products and regions, and support data-driven business decisions through an interactive Power BI dashboard.

---

## 🎯 Objectives

- Analyze electronic sales performance across **multiple regions and product categories**
- Visualize key trends and performance metrics through **interactive charts and graphs**
- Identify **top-performing products and regions** based on revenue and sales volume
- Develop a **comprehensive KPI dashboard** to monitor business-critical metrics
- Automate **monthly reporting** to streamline performance analysis

## 📊 Dashboard Features

| Feature | Description |
|---|---|
| **Sales Overview** | Total revenue, units sold, and order count across time periods |
| **Regional Analysis** | Sales performance breakdown by region with comparative metrics |
| **Product Category Analysis** | Revenue and volume by electronics category |
| **Top Performers** | Ranked view of best-selling products and highest-revenue regions |
| **Trend Analysis** | Monthly/quarterly sales trends to identify growth patterns |
| **KPI Cards** | At-a-glance metrics for quick business decision-making |
| **Automated Monthly Report** | Dynamic date filters enabling automated monthly performance views |

## 📈 Key Metrics Tracked

- **Total Revenue** — Aggregate sales across all regions and categories
- **Units Sold** — Volume-based performance by product and region
- **Month-over-Month (MoM) Growth** — Trend tracking for performance benchmarking
- **Top 5 Products** — Best-performing products by revenue
- **Regional Contribution %** — Each region's share of total sales
- **Average Order Value (AOV)** — Revenue efficiency metric

---

## 🔍 Key Insights

- Identified top-performing product categories and their revenue contribution
- Highlighted underperforming regions to support targeted business strategies
- Tracked monthly sales trends to detect seasonal patterns
- Enabled stakeholders to monitor KPIs in real time through dynamic filters
